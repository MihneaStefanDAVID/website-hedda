<div>Filming is how I look at space when I am not drawing it.</div>
<div>Some of these videos started in a course, others simply in my free time: the daily train ride home, a hidden courtyard in Zurich, people moving through the Kunsthaus, small moments with the people around me.</div>
<div>What connects them are the same questions I bring to architecture: how we move through a place, how routines shape it, how sound changes the way we perceive it, and how the people who use a space are often more telling than the space itself. I enjoy capturing these moments, and they keep feeding back into the way I design.</div>
